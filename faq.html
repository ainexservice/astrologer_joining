Application Process

How can I apply to join BABA ASTRO?
Visit our Apply Now page and complete the application with accurate contact and professional information. The online form must be connected to the submission system before applications can actually be saved.
What information should I prepare?
Prepare your name, contact details, location, relevant experience, specializations, languages and preferred consultation methods. Only provide information that is necessary for your application.
What happens after I submit my application?
Applications may be checked for completeness and suitability. If your application progresses, the team can communicate the next steps and any applicable requirements.
How long does application review take?
Review times may vary. A specific timeline has not been established here, so please use the official contact channel for updates once it is available.

Eligibility and Expertise

Which specializations can I apply with?
You can indicate relevant expertise such as Vedic Astrology, Kundli Analysis, Tarot Reading, Numerology, Vastu or other related areas in which you have appropriate knowledge.
Can beginners apply?
You can provide your actual level of experience in the application. Eligibility criteria and selection requirements must be confirmed by the BABA ASTRO team.
Do I need a professional qualification?
Add any relevant training, qualifications or experience you genuinely have. Any specific documentation requirements should be confirmed before onboarding.
Does submitting an application guarantee selection?
No. Submitting an application expresses your interest. Selection and onboarding may depend on review and applicable eligibility requirements.

Consultation Preferences

Which consultation methods can I select?
The application lets you indicate preferences such as phone, chat, video or multiple methods. Actual availability depends on the services BABA ASTRO offers and confirms.
Can I mention more than one language?
Yes. Select your primary language and enter any other languages you can comfortably use for professional communication.
Can I specify my preferred working hours?
You can indicate your general availability in the application. Final schedules, if applicable, should be discussed during onboarding.

Payments and Partnership Terms

Is any income guaranteed?
No specific earnings, customer volume or amount of work is guaranteed. Please review all applicable rates, fees, payment terms and responsibilities before agreeing to a partnership.
Are there any joining fees?
Do not assume that a fee applies or that joining is free without official confirmation. Ask for written details of any applicable fees and terms before making a payment.
When will I be able to start consultations?
Any start date depends on the application review, onboarding and the availability of the relevant service. Submission alone does not confirm a start date.

Privacy and Support

How will my application information be used?
Application information should be used only for stated purposes, such as reviewing your application and contacting you about it, according to the published Privacy Policy.
How can I contact BABA ASTRO?
Visit the Contact Us page for available contact details. Confirm that the published email address or phone number is correct before relying on it.
What if I entered incorrect information?
Contact the official support channel once it is available and explain which details need correction. Avoid submitting multiple applications unless instructed to do so.
